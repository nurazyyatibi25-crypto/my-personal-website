My Hometown: Semporna

Located on the southeast coast of Sabah, Malaysia, Semporna is a coastal town and a gateway to some of the region's well-known islands and marine attractions.

Kredit photo Waterfront: Fabio Achilli, lesen CC BY 2.0..

About the town

Where the sea meets island life

Semporna is widely known for its coastal scenery, nearby islands, clear waters and marine biodiversity. The town is an important starting point for visitors exploring the surrounding islands and diving areas.

Its connection to the sea is an important part of local life, including fishing, tourism and the movement of people and goods between the mainland and nearby islands.

Why I love Semporna

I appreciate Semporna for its beautiful natural surroundings, peaceful sea views and the special feeling of being connected to the coast. It is a place that reminds me of the beauty of nature.

“The ocean makes me feel calm, grateful and inspired.”
